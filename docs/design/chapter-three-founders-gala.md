# Chapter 3 Act III — The Founders' Gala

## Purpose

The Founders' Gala closes Chapter 3 by converting the ranch's exhibition placement and selected patron charter into a visible town-wide legacy. It is a deterministic, non-combat, non-failing public event staged at Founders' Plaza.

## Eligibility

The act begins only after **The Patron Circuit** is complete and a permanent patron has been recorded.

The selected patron is inherited from Act II and cannot be changed during the gala.

## Progression

1. Review the Founders' Plaza invitation.
2. Choose one of two patron-specific gala plans.
3. Host the gala and permanently record its deterministic score and outcome.
4. Advance to the next Ranch Day.
5. Read the council report and establish the permanent civic legacy.

All stages persist in `chapterThreeFoundersGalaV1`.

## Patron plans

Every route has one free plan and one stronger paid plan. The free plan prevents resource-locking.

### Registry Sponsorship

- **Open Ledger Showcase** — Free, +8 preparation score.
- **Patrons' Banquet** — 75 Gold, +16 preparation score.

### Petra's Works Charter

- **Volunteer Works Fair** — Free, +8 preparation score.
- **Showcase Pavilion** — 4 Materials, +16 preparation score.

### Rose Lantern Hospitality Charter

- **Public Salon Evening** — Free, +8 preparation score.
- **Lantern Reception** — 1 Rumor Token, +16 preparation score.

The Rose Lantern route remains adult-only, optional, and consent-first. Its gala content is public and non-intimate. House-rule acknowledgment is required, while romantic or sexual participation is never required.

## Deterministic score

The 0–100 score combines:

- 34-point public-event foundation;
- up to 24 points from the Act I Guild Exhibition score;
- up to 18 points from patron-specific standing;
- up to 10 points from the exhibition representative's level and Affection;
- 8 or 16 preparation points from the selected plan.

Patron standing uses:

- Registry: Guild Points and completed contracts;
- Builder: completed Builder projects and stored Materials;
- Rose Lantern: House Trust and Rumor Tokens.

The result is recorded when the gala begins. Reloading cannot reroll it or avoid a paid plan's cost.

## Outcomes

### Community Gala

- Minimum score: 0
- 180 Gold
- 3 Guild Points
- 3 Materials
- 10 Town Prestige
- Representative: +20 XP, +2 Affection

### Celebrated Gala

- Minimum score: 60
- 300 Gold
- 5 Guild Points
- 5 Materials
- 15 Town Prestige
- Representative: +30 XP, +3 Affection

### Landmark Gala

- Minimum score: 78
- 450 Gold
- 7 Guild Points
- 8 Materials
- 20 Town Prestige
- Representative: +45 XP, +4 Affection

Every outcome advances and completes the chapter.

## Shared civic legacy

Every completed gala grants:

- +2% Gold on unfinished current and future weekly Guild contracts;
- 3% lower Gold and Materials costs for future Builder projects;
- +5 Gold on future Rose Lantern hospitality shifts.

## Patron-strengthened legacy

### Registry route

- Guild legacy becomes +5% Gold total.
- Adds +1 Guild Point per affected weekly contract.

### Builder route

- Gala Builder discount becomes 8%.
- This stacks with Petra's 10% Act II charter for 18% total.

### Rose Lantern route

- Gala hospitality bonus becomes +10 Gold.
- Adds +1 House Trust and +1 Rumor Token per shift.
- This stacks with the Act II charter.

## Idempotency

- Plan costs are charged once.
- Score and outcome are recorded once.
- The council report can be finalized once.
- Guild bonuses use a separate weekly application marker.
- Builder affordability and deductions use the same displayed effective cost.
- Hospitality bonuses are added once after the base shift resolves.

## Presentation

- The Ranch Hub advances from Act II to the Act III launcher.
- Founders' Plaza appears on the town map after eligibility.
- Both launchers open the same responsive journal and progression interface.
- Local SVG artwork is used for the story hero and town location.

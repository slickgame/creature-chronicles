# Coliseum C3 — Marks, Combat Loot, Exclusive Moves, and Creature Contracts

## Purpose

C3 adds a persistent reward economy on top of the C2 authored 3v3 circuit without replacing the C2 battle, XP, division, record, or opponent systems.

The new systems are:

- Coliseum Marks
- deterministic post-battle loot
- a Marks Exchange
- offense, defense, and utility equipment slots
- four dedicated Coliseum moves
- one-time creature recruitment contracts
- a capacity-safe Recruitment Hold
- a Marks and loot ledger

## Save model

C3 stores its versioned state in the save flag:

```text
coliseumC3StateV1
```

The state contains:

- current Marks
- processed C2 result IDs
- shop purchase counts
- dedicated technique-manual stock
- pending contract IDs
- recruited contract IDs
- recent Marks and loot history
- whether the legacy stipend was applied

Malformed or missing C3 JSON normalizes to a safe empty state. C2 progress remains stored independently in `coliseumProgressV2`.

## Result synchronization

C3 does not change the C2 result-recording boundary. Instead, the C3 shell observes the permanent C2 history and processes every new result ID exactly once.

A processed result cannot grant duplicate:

- Marks
- Materials
- Outfitter items
- reward-ledger entries

Losses and draws are marked processed but do not award Marks or C3 combat loot.

## Marks awards

Victory awards depend on the encounter division.

| Division | Repeat win | First-clear bonus | Division-champion bonus |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Novice | 2 | +3 | +2 |
| Bronze | 3 | +4 | +3 |
| Silver | 4 | +6 | +4 |
| Crown | 6 | +9 | +5 |

A first-clear division champion therefore awards the repeat amount, first-clear bonus, and champion bonus together.

## Legacy stipend

C1/C2 encounters that were already completed but have no C2 history entry receive a one-time stipend of 2 Marks per migrated clear.

The stipend:

- is applied once
- does not recreate old loot
- does not reopen first-clear rewards
- does not relock any encounter

## Combat loot

### First clears

Every first clear grants Materials equal to division order plus one:

- Novice: 2 Materials
- Bronze: 3 Materials
- Silver: 4 Materials
- Crown: 5 Materials

First-clear division champions also grant one existing Battle Outfitter item:

- Novice champion: Field Tonic
- Bronze champion: Team Tactics Kit
- Silver champion: Focus Manual
- Crown champion: Revival Salve

### Repeat victories

Repeat loot uses the permanent result ID as a deterministic seed:

- 12%: one Field Tonic
- next 9%: two Materials
- next 4%: one Focus Manual
- otherwise: no bonus loot

Reloading cannot reroll the result.

## Marks Exchange

The Exchange includes five reward groups:

- consumables
- equipment
- technique manuals
- creature contracts
- prestige

Rewards may require a specific C2 encounter clear. Purchase limits are stored per reward ID.

Marks purchases never use Gold, Guild Points, or Materials.

## Coliseum moves

C3 adds four non-inheritable moves with `sourceType: "coliseum"`.

### Arena Breaker

- Physical, single enemy
- 34 power
- 93 accuracy
- 12 Battle Energy
- 2-round cooldown
- breaks Guarded through the existing Guard Break tag

### Tactical Reversal

- Support, self
- grants Guarded for one round
- grants Inspired for two rounds
- priority 2

### Arena Medic

- Healing, single ally
- 32 base healing
- cleanses Slowed
- cleanses Weakened

### Champion Command

- Support, all allies
- grants Inspired for two rounds
- restores 12 Battle Energy to every living ally
- Crown-only Exchange unlock

All five current creature families are compatible. These moves cannot be learned from Focus Manuals, inherited through breeding, or generated by move-combination recipes.

A purchased dedicated manual teaches one creature and is consumed only after a successful learning operation. Full eight-move libraries require a legal replacement. The required basic move and native species signature remain protected.

## Recruitment Hold and habitat capacity

Purchasing a contract does not immediately create a creature.

The contract enters Recruitment Hold. Redemption checks the matching family habitat:

- habitat exists
- habitat is unlocked
- current occupants are below capacity
- the deterministic contract creature ID is not already present

When full, the contract remains owned and pending. Marks are not lost again, and no creature is auto-deleted or placed beyond capacity.

When space is available, redemption atomically adds:

- the creature record
- the creature ID
- the matching habitat membership
- the recruited-contract record

The contract can never be redeemed twice.

## Player-facing screens

The C3 shell adds persistent navigation between:

- Arena
- Marks Exchange
- Technique Desk
- Recruitment Hold
- Reward Ledger
- Battle Outfitter
- Town

The existing C2 arena remains the combat and encounter screen underneath the C3 shell.

The Battle Outfitter now displays:

- standard Gold/Materials shelves
- complete standard and Marks-exclusive stock
- offense, defense, and utility slots
- all assignable equipment
- Focus Training
- the existing move-training overlay

Town shows a C3 quick-access badge with Marks, clear count, and pending contracts.

## Deliberately deferred to C4

C3 does not add:

- daily challenges
- gauntlets
- boss trials outside the permanent circuit
- partial recovery between battles
- rotating modifiers
- weekly leaderboards
- auto-resolve

Those belong to Coliseum C4.

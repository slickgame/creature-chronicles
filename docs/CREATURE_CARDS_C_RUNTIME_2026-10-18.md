# Creature Cards C

The user selected C's gallery and inspector, with an explicit Open profile button on desktop. CreatureCardsC replaces the remaining generic parchment Cards dialog. The exact concept board remains at public/images/ui/creature-cards-review/options.png. The asset map and reproducible frame generator are documented in public/images/ui/creature-cards-c/README.md.

Desktop pairs paged portrait tiles with a selected inspector: canonical portrait, identity, rarity/generation, live status, exact Energy, Affection and Fertility, giver/receiver readiness, last-bred record, favorite/lock actions, Card details and prominent Open profile. Phone uses Gallery and Selected creature views, a selected strip, explicit paging and Open profile in both relevant views. Short screens use compact summaries and full paged Card details. Fertility is a value/grade, not a percentage meter.

Tile preview, paging, search, tabs, details and cancellation remain read-only. Opening Cards carries the picker preview without changing the underlying profile. Open profile confirms the exact selected creature and retains the existing profile section. Favorite and lock use their existing explicit save actions. Shared Filters C keeps draft/apply/cancel behavior; shared Compare A preselects the previewed creature. Full Card details retain variant/species, shiny state, exact Energy and percentage, Affection, Fertility value/grade, complete giver/receiver blockers, last-bred history, rarity, generation, protection and favorite state. No binary sex controls/symbols, new breeding mechanics or artwork substitution.

PR21 stays draft and unmerged. Actual desktop/phone screenshots and verification results are recorded separately under docs/validation.

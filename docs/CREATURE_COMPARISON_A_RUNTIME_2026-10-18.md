# Creature comparison A

The user selected A and requested production assets and visual fidelity for fonts, layout, sizes, spacing, frames and icons. CreatureCompareChooserA and CreatureComparisonA replace the old comparison chooser/result only; the separate generic Cards gallery remains available. The exact comparison board is retained in public/images/ui/creature-compare-review/options.png. See the production asset map at public/images/ui/creature-compare-a/README.md.

The chooser uses a paged searchable list, two persistent selected slots with Remove controls, selection count and fixed Compare/Back controls. It requires two distinct currently owned creatures, preserves selections across searches/pages, and disables additional unselected choices until a slot is removed. Profile More actions preselects its creature. Change creatures returns with both selections intact. Browsing does not persist or modify creatures. Inherited roster filters remain respected.

The ledger opens on Stats & Talents, showing permanent attributes and innate grades separately; higher current numeric values are accented, with ties neutral. Overview retains level, generation, rarity, exact Energy, Affection, status and protection. Breeding has paged role/readiness, Fertility, Affection and individual histories; full blocker reasons remain in eligibility details. Pair record keeps familiarity, lineage and the existing most-recent recorded breeding summary. Setup buttons route to the existing breeding screen with explicit giver/receiver IDs, without performing breeding.

Talent summaries use canonical owned talent names/grades/icons, and the focused viewer keeps all talents available through paging. Full details reuse the existing TalentDetails component and presentation engine, including active/planned/legacy distinctions and actual applicability/conditions. No fictional concept talent or approximate effect text enters runtime.

Phones retain all Overview / Stats & Talents / Breeding tabs. Tables page by available height, and talent summaries become a focused action on phones/short windows. Complete text remains accessible through pages; no binary sex symbols/labels, invented scores, equipment additions to attributes, or new breeding mechanics. Back to roster preserves the underlying selected creature/profile tab. PR21 stays draft and unmerged.

# Choose Egg — approved B Open Ledger

The user selected B from the Choose Egg concept board, with the explicit correction that the heading must not sit on the center crease. NurseryEggPickerB uses two page-aligned header columns: Back on the left, Choose egg wholly on the right. It reuses nursery-ledger-c/open-ledger.webp, the canonical species eggs and licensed Tinos fonts. No new raster artwork is required.

One egg is previewed per spread. The original sorted egg collection, draft ID, explicit Select egg, Cancel and parent selection callbacks remain authoritative. Previous/Next and the multi-egg selector only change the draft. Empty or removed drafts disable confirmation. Names, species, type, rarity and incubation come from actual records; ready eggs retain explicit readiness. Browsing does not spend resources, hatch, reroll or write game state.

The layout reserves the parchment gutters, lower page rules and botanical corner. The heading remains in its right-page cell on compact layouts as well. Original Nursery/Atelier/inheritance screens and their transactions are unchanged.

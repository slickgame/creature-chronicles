# Tactical battle B, with A's art scale and impact numbers

Approved direction: B's tactical ribbon and group-target preview, A's larger full-body characters and unboxed damage numbers. User requested selecting a move, clicking a creature to target it, then clicking that same target again to queue; two visible status effects and an ellipsis opening all active effects.

## Behavior

- Explicitly choosing a move arms target-click queuing. The first click selects; the next click on the same target queues only if the move is usable. Changing moves, actor, category/page, closing the dock, queuing or confirming clears the armed click. The existing Queue Move button remains available. Native buttons support touch and keyboard.
- Valid targets have dashed gold rings. Selected targets have solid gold rings and TARGET labels; an area move highlights every normalized living target. Self/ally/field moves retain the existing targeting rules. Taunted single-enemy moves reject incompatible clicked enemies instead of silently previewing one creature while queuing another.
- At most two active status entries appear beside each creature, with remaining rounds. More than two adds a literal ellipsis button with an accessible total count. Any badge or ellipsis opens every active effect, including stat-specific labels, duration, stacks, amount, source and existing mechanics. Inspection pauses playback so effects do not disappear while being read; closing leaves Resume available.
- The parchment ribbon separates First/Next during planning from Acting Now/Up Next during recorded playback. Only the current revealed move appears. Remaining actors retain numbered portraits. Recovery has its own label.
- Wider/taller desktop formations retain uncropped canonical art. White, outlined HP impact numbers float without a filled box; healing/energy and status/miss/KO use distinct colors/text. Full-color playback and per-effect health updates are preserved. Phone formations remain stacked to fit the viewport.

Row-major cells: Bleed droplet, Stun stars, Guarded shield; Inspired sun, Marked crosshair, Taunted speech bubble; Exhausted energy vial, Weakened broken sword, Slowed snail. Actual alpha transparency. One generated atlas, exported at 768×768 WebP for production; the original PNG remains in generated_images. CSS cell positioning avoids per-icon crop files. No canonical creature art was generated or edited.

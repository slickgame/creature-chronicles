# Resource economy: equipment clarification and next pass

Approved constraint: buying finished equipment from a shop costs Gold. Do not convert shop gear bills to materials. Commissioning a blacksmith to craft equipment can cost named materials plus a Gold labor fee. Keep existing purchased gear and saved ownership intact.

Proposed first pass (not implemented):

- Shop: predictable ready-made stock, Gold only, immediate purchase.
- Blacksmith: fixed recipes using planks, iron/steel and other named resources plus Gold. Exact output grade and stats visible before confirmation. No random failure or surprise fees initially.
- Balance crafting by valuing player materials and labor together, modestly below equivalent retail prices. Do not give crafting a hidden extra stat bonus; unique recipes can distinguish it later.
- Mission board: mostly Gold and GP, with occasional clearly listed ingredient/component rewards. Requests name exact resources and preserve reserved stock.
- NPC rewards: resources tied to each NPC's role and quests; retain relationship rewards.
- Battle: Gold/GP and mode currencies remain; named materials are supplemental drops where sensible, not universal replacements for money.
- Food/material bundles list exact quantities. Planned icons: milk, eggs, meat, stone, iron; use the established painterly inventory style.

Implement named bundles/rewards and art first. Add the blacksmith after agreeing on location, starter recipes and labor prices. Store equipment prices remain Gold throughout.

Its low-contrast text and older outline symbols need to match the updated interiors.

## Content rules after selection

- Preserve actual generation, origin, saved parent names, notes and lineage markers. Missing ancestry means no parent records; never infer a complete safe genealogy.
- Present the stored risk as Birth assessment or similarly explicit historical wording. It reflects the contributor relationship recorded at hatching. Any new breeding pair needs a fresh assessment.
- Parent names may outlive an owned creature. Show a name record without a broken View parent action when no matching owned record can be resolved.
- Do not zip parent IDs and names blindly: hatching filters creature IDs, while names can also contain a player contributor. Resolve from snapshots/birth records when available, otherwise preserve names without speculative links.
- Lineage markers are saved lineage/mutation records, not new talents or skills. No new inheritance odds, breeding bonuses or progression.
- Keep family details read-only, preserve portrait/navigation/footer, and use pages or focused views on small screens.
